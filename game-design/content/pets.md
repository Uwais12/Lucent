# Spirits (Pets) — Lucent: Shards of the Shattered Sun

> 12 launch Spirits. Power-budget share: **5% of total combat power at endgame** (per `design/04-progression-and-economy.md` §2). One active Spirit slot. Each Spirit grants a **passive** while equipped and a **triggered active** on a long cooldown.
>
> Spirits are fragments of pre-Dimming Iridian fauna that survived because a light-shard bonded to them — the bearer's smaller cousins. Build-affinity references the 8 hero archetypes from `design/03-world-and-theme.md`: **Dawnbow, Wardlight, Spinekind, Embercaller, Frostshard, Prismborn, Wraithsworn, Sunkin Heir**. Spirits are hero-agnostic; "affinity" indicates which archetype's build pattern they amplify most.

# Balance Checks

### 1. Power budget — Spirits sum to ~5% at endgame

A maxed Legendary Spirit at L80 6★ contributes ~+12% damage passively + a triggered active firing ~12× per 5-min run. Passive yields ~2–3% of total combat power; active ~3–4%. Sum: **~5%**, matching the §2 budget. Across rarities equipped: Common ~3%, Rare ~4%, Epic ~4.5%, Legendary ~5%, Mythic ~6%. The 5% target holds at the median; Mythic occupies its expected ceiling.

### 2. No Spirit active outclasses a Legendary in-run ability

15 Legendary in-run abilities at launch typically grant permanent run-wide effects (e.g. *"+30% damage for the run"* or *"+2 projectiles permanent"*). Spirit actives are **time-windowed** equivalents at lower magnitude:

- Mosslet's Greenward (8%/s for 4s every 25s) < Legendary Holy Aura (10%/s permanent).
- Prismfox's burst (+2 proj for 4s) < Legendary Multishot (+2 permanent).
- Wraithpup's Soulbite (15% heal/cast) < Legendary Lifesteal Aura (8% lifesteal permanent).
- Hollow Owl's Eyestrike (820% ATK, 26s CD) ≈ one cast of a Legendary nuke; far less DPS over time than sustained Energy Beam.
- **Solchild's Suncall** (1600% ATK + invuln, 35s CD) is the strongest single active but trails a sustained Legendary Energy Beam in cumulative damage; Mythic + anniversary-only gating justifies the ceiling.

Spirits **augment** builds; they do not **substitute** for Legendary picks.

### 3. ≥3 free-path Spirits remain endgame-competitive

Free-path Spirits (Common + Rare): Glimwing, Spinekitten, Mosslet, Prismfox, Wraithpup, Frost Yeti-cub. **Four are endgame-competitive at 6★** in at least one meta build:

- **Spinekitten** — Stormpelt's lightning stack is a Spinekind keystone.
- **Mosslet** — Eldermoss's persistent Greenward is a guild-boss MVP for Wardlight/Wraithsworn sustain.
- **Frost Yeti-cub** — Avalanche Calf is the cheapest freeze-stack source, required for Frostshard's signature.
- **Glimwing** — Wakemoth's Lucent Trail scales hard in Tower/Survival modes.

Exceeds the 3-minimum target. The lone soft warning: **Sunkin Heir** is the archetype most reliant on gacha Spirits (Sunkin Carp, Dimcaller Raven). Mitigation: Sunkin Carp is featured on the launch banner — reachable in ~10 pulls via soft pity, and also stocked in the anniversary token shop.

### 4. Active cooldown sanity

CDs span **18–35s**. Per 5-min run, every Spirit fires **8–16 times** — agentic, not screen-flooding (preserves Pillar 8 readability). Slower CDs (26–35s) are reserved for the highest-impact actives (Hollow Owl, Dimcaller Raven, Solchild).
