## 0. How to read this document

Lucent runs **five concurrent LiveOps layers** at all times (per Pillar 4 — "a new event tab item every login"):

1. **Daily layer** — always-on, resets 09:00 player-local.
2. **Weekly layer** — always-on, resets Mon 09:00 player-local.
3. **Battle Pass** — 45-day season; two seasons fit in a 90-day window (S7 days 1-45, S8 days 46-90).
4. **Monthly anchor "The Long Dawn"** — 30-day overlay that re-skins three times per quarter (Chapter A / B / C).
5. **Special events** — 1-2 stacked at any time. Tower of Light, Banner Festival, Lucent Tournament, Realm Reckoning, IP Collab.

The grid in §1 is the centerpiece. The detail specs (§2), push plan (§3), monetization beats (§4), content cadence (§5), and year-1 tentpoles (§6) hang off it.

### Legend — codes used in the grid

**Daily resets** (every day, same code, just listed once at top for brevity):
- `DQ` — 6 daily quests refresh at 09:00 local
- `LOG-Dn` — Day-n login-calendar claim (30-day stamp card; resets every 30 days)
- `FC` — Free chest (24h cooldown, ad-watchable for 2nd claim)
- `ADx5` — 5 ad-watch reward slots (chest, +5 energy ×4, daily spin)
- `DD` — Daily Deal $0.99
- `DPD` — Daily Premium Deal $2.99
- `PSx3` — Free pet-shard spin (3/day)

**Weekly events:**
- `WM-Wn` — 7 Weekly Missions, Week n of the season
- `WB-A/B/C` — Weekly Boss (rotates: Aurelion Husk / Bramblelord / Storm-Echo / Crawler Queen; one per week)
- `ARN-R` — PvP Arena soft reset (Monday)
- `GD` — Guild donations cap reset
- `GB-x` — Guild Boss rotation (Wed start; Boss-1, Boss-2, Boss-3)
- `TF` — Tower-mode floor-pack drop (every Friday; +5 new floors to endless tower)

**Battle Pass:**
- `BP-Sn:Tk` — Battle Pass Season n, milestone Tier k
- `BP-S7` running D1-45, `BP-S8` running D46-90
- Tier milestones called out: T15 (premium hero unlock), T35 (free hero unlock), T50 (final cosmetic + pet variant)

**Monthly anchor:**
- `LD-A` / `LD-B` / `LD-C` — The Long Dawn, Chapter A (D1-30), Chapter B (D31-60), Chapter C (D61-90)
- Sub-quest gates: `LD-G1` (token milestone 1), `LD-G2`, `LD-G3`, `LD-G4` per chapter

**Special events:**
- `TOL` — Tower of Light (7-day PvE escalating tower)
- `BAN` — Banner Festival (5-day gacha rate-up + double pulls)
- `TRN` — Lucent Tournament (3-day async 1v1 bracket)
- `RR-V` / `RR-E` / `RR-T` — Realm Reckoning chapters: Verdant Reach / Emberwild Steppe / Tidal Spire
- `COL` — IP Collab window (placeholder; replaced with named partner when secured)

### Rhythm check (per Pillar 4)

Every day the player opens the app, they see **at minimum 4 concurrent surfaces**:

- Daily quests / login (always).
- Weekly missions + weekly boss (always).
- Battle pass progression (always, since S7 and S8 cover the entire 90 days continuously).
- Monthly anchor "The Long Dawn" (always, since LD-A/B/C cover D1-90 continuously).

On **70 of 90 days** there is also at least one special event live (special A or B column non-empty). Largest gap between special-event-starts: **8 days** (D28 → D36), still inside the "24h between event-ends and next event-start" tolerance from `liveops-retention.md`, because LD-A wrap rewards land at D30 and TOL-B starts D36, with LD-B opening at D31 carrying the slack.

## 2. Event Detail Specs (one page each)

### 2.1 Tower of Light (`TOL`)

**Format.** Endless PvE escalating tower variant. 50 procedurally-modified floors per run, hard-curve modifier per floor (e.g., "all-fire-damage," "no-dodge," "double-loot"). Distinct from the always-on Tower mode: TOL uses Tower mode's combat core but locks a **special event modifier set** that does not appear elsewhere.

**Duration.** 7 days. Runs three times per 90-day window at exactly 35-day intervals (D1-7, D36-42, D71-77). Matches "every 5 weeks."

**Mechanics.**
- 3 attempts/day (bankable up to 6).
- Cumulative damage / floor reached drives a leaderboard, bracketed at 100-player shards.
- Checkpoint reward chests every 5 floors: F5, F10, F15, F20, F25, F30, F40, F50.
- Final-day leaderboard settles 23:59 player-local on D7; mail Mon morning.

**Rewards.**
- Free track: Sigil Dust, Embers, BP Pass Points, 1 hero shard at F30.
- Top-1k bracket: Legendary gear chest, 1500 gems, "Lightbearer" frame cosmetic.
- Top-100: Mythic-tier gear chest, 3000 gems, exclusive aura cosmetic.
- Top-10: above + season-anchored title and animated icon.

**Monetization hooks.**
- **TOL Climb Pack** $4.99: +2 daily attempts ×7 days, 2× checkpoint gold.
- **TOL Spire Bundle** $14.99: above + Legendary chest day-1 + 1500 gems.
- **TOL Tickets** $0.99 (cap 3): +1 attempt on the spot.
- Ad-watch: +1 attempt/day on top of the 3 base.

**Target ARPDAU lift.** +18-22% during the 7-day window vs. baseline. (Per `liveops-retention.md` §4.3: Sky/Cake Tower is among the strongest single special-event surfaces in the genre.)

### 2.2 Banner Festival (`BAN`)

**Format.** 5-day gacha rate-up event on the Wish hero banner. Featured hero gets 0.5% → 1.5% Mythic-roll boost; **double-pull weekend** on D4-5 (2 pulls for the gem cost of 1, ad-gated, cap 10/day).

**Duration.** 5 days. Runs three times per 90-day window roughly every 4 weeks (D11-15, D39-43, D67-71). Light overlap with TOL/COL is intentional — banner spend pairs well with another active goal.

**Mechanics.**
- Per Pillar 5, **all rates published in-app** at banner-entry screen; no obfuscation.
- Pity carries across Banner Festival re-runs of the same hero, **resets** on hero swap.
- 10-pull soft pity (Epic+ guaranteed), 50-pull hard pity (Mythic guaranteed with 50% rate-up chance, mercy on next).
- Daily free pull (via ad) for all 5 days — non-payers feel the event.

**Rewards (event store, stocked for 5 days only).**
- Banner-hero shards (200 / 1000 / 5000 gems).
- "Festival" cosmetic frame + chat bubble.
- Limited Lucent Shard packs (event-only conversion rate: 1.2× usual).

**Monetization hooks.**
- **Banner Starter** $4.99: 10 pulls + 1 featured shard. One-time per event.
- **Banner Surge** $19.99: 30 pulls + 5 featured shards + cosmetic frame. One-time per event.
- **Banner Whale** $99.99: 80 pulls + guaranteed Mythic-rate-up shard + animated icon. One-time per event.
- Always-on tier-skip from gem packs (no event-exclusive gem SKU; gem packs are evergreen).

**Target ARPDAU lift.** +35-50% during the 5-day window. Banner Festival is the single highest-impulse-spend special in the calendar.

### 2.3 Lucent Tournament (`TRN`)

**Format.** 3-day async 1v1 PvP bracket layered on top of the always-on Arena. **Fixed-loadout** mode: every player uses an event-balanced hero pool and a pre-set ability deck — skill differentiator is talent grid + rune optimization, not gear gap. Removes pay-to-win optics; rewards mechanical skill.

**Duration.** 3 days. Runs twice per 90-day window at 42-day intervals (D20-22, D62-64). Matches "every 6 weeks."

**Mechanics.**
- 5 entry tickets/day base + 2 ad-watch + 3 purchasable.
- Single-elimination 16-player bracket, generated daily from MMR-banded shards (~500 players per shard).
- Daily bracket settles 22:00 player-local; player advances to next-day "promoted shard" if they won D-1.
- Final settlement D3 23:59. Top-3 of each shard get the headline rewards.

**Rewards.**
- Participation: 100 gems + Pass Points + 1 hero shard (rotates).
- Daily-bracket top-3: 500 gems + Embers chest.
- Final top-3: 3000 gems + Mythic gear chest + season-rotated "Champion" emote.
- Final top-1 of any shard: above + animated weapon trail cosmetic.

**Monetization hooks.**
- **Tournament Pack** $4.99: 10 extra tickets + 1 rune chest. One-time per event.
- **Bracket Insurance** $2.99: re-enter once after first loss (cap 1/event).
- No pay-to-win — tickets buy access, not power.

**Target ARPDAU lift.** +10-14% during the 3-day window. Lower than banner because no power-purchase, but high engagement (D-1 → D+1 retention bump +6-8 pp during event week per genre benchmarks).

### 2.4 Realm Reckoning (`RR-V` / `RR-E` / `RR-T` …)

**Format.** Chapter-themed PvE event tied to the 7 realms of the Shattered Sun story (Verdant Reach, Emberwild Steppe, Tidal Spire, Skyspire Wastes, Stormhollow, Mirror Reach, Ashen Cradle). Each realm gets one 10-day Reckoning per year; **rotation cycles all 7 over 7 months**, so within any 90-day window we see 3 chapters.

**Duration.** 10 days each. Runs 3× per 90-day quarter (D23-32, D53-62, D80-89). The cadence shifts by ~3 days each quarter so all 7 realms cycle in 7 months and rejoin the calendar's anniversary slot.

**Mechanics.**
- New PvE "expedition" mode — biome-themed boss with realm-flavor mechanics (Verdant: vine-trap arenas; Emberwild: lava-flow telegraphs; Tidal: tide-cycle hazards).
- 15-node milestone track ("pass-the-checkpoint," per `liveops-retention.md` §4.4). Tokens earned passively from any run, doubled from the new biome mode.
- Boss has 3-phase fight unique to the realm. Damage-tier rewards at 5M / 25M / 100M cumulative across the 10 days.

**Rewards.**
- Realm-themed cosmetic skin for one launch hero (rotates).
- Realm-specific pet (long-tail collectible — 7 in the set across the year).
- Lucent Shards, hero shards (rotated to a launch-hero, not the current season hero).
- "Realm Banner" wall-art for the home screen (cosmetic) at the final milestone.

**Monetization hooks.**
- **Realm Pack — Verdant / Emberwild / Tidal / etc.** $9.99: realm cosmetic + 1000 gems + token boost. One-time per realm appearance.
- **Realm Premium** $19.99: above + realm-pet shard + +25% token rate.
- **Token-Skip** $4.99: skip-to-next-milestone (cap 2 per event).

**Target ARPDAU lift.** +20-25% during the 10-day window. Realm Reckoning is the long-tail completionist hook — collectors track all 7.

### 2.5 IP Collab (`COL`)

**Format.** Branded crossover event. Placeholder in the template — replaced with named partner art/copy when deal-flow lands. 10 days, every 90 days (slot reserved D46-55 of the template). If no IP secured for a given quarter, slot is filled with an **expanded Realm Reckoning** (12 days instead of 10) or a **double Banner Festival**.

**Duration.** 10 days. Once per 90-day window.

**Mechanics.**
- Themed boss + themed map skin (re-skin existing combat content with IP art).
- Collab hero (loaned, not permanent — returns to a "vault" 12 months out for re-runs per `liveops-retention.md` §3.4).
- Themed login mini-calendar (5 days) inside the 10-day window.
- App-icon swap option for opt-in users (Apple icon variants).

**Rewards.**
- Free track: collab cosmetic frame, collab pet shards, collab hero shards (slow path to unlock at end of event).
- Paid track: 25-tier mini-pass at $9.99 — guaranteed collab hero unlock at tier 20.

**Monetization hooks.**

## 5. Content Cadence Map (LiveOps ↔ Production)

Per `design/01-core-loop-spec.md` §8, the production team owes LiveOps the following on the 90-day rhythm. LiveOps owes production a content lock 6 weeks before each window.

| Cadence | Asset | Per 90-day window | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Every 4 weeks** | 1 new hero (free + premium path, shards via campaign + season banner) | 3 heroes per quarter | BP season hero (×2), one realm-rotation hero |
| **Every 4 weeks** | 1 new pet (with rarity ladder + ascension art) | 3 pets per quarter | BP season pet variant (×2), realm pet (×3 — Verdant/Emberwild/Tidal) |
| **Every 2 weeks** | 5-10 new abilities + 1 new gear set | ~6 ability drops, 6 gear sets per quarter | Drops into the always-on pool + event-currency-store rotations |
| **Every 6-8 weeks** | 1 new chapter / biome (5 rooms or 1 boss + 4 rooms) + 1 music track | ~1.5 new chapters per quarter | Realm Reckoning biome content + LD-A/B/C banner art |
| **Every 2 weeks** | Balance patch (talent / gear / hero tuning) | ~6 balance patches per quarter | Slotted on Tuesdays so post-Monday reset is clean |
| **Every 30 days** | LD chapter (banner art, sub-quest scripts, event-store item rotation) | 3 LD chapters per quarter | Monthly anchor swap |
| **Every 45 days** | Battle Pass season (theme, 50 free + 50 premium tier rewards, season hero + skin + pet variant) | 2 BP seasons per quarter | Battle Pass swap |
| **Every 90 days** | Collab partner art + hero kit + map re-skin + 5-day login mini-calendar art | 1 collab per quarter (or substitution per §2.5) | IP collab slot |
| **Every 90 days** | Cinematic trailer for Q+1 reveal at D90 | 1 trailer per quarter | Q-end push and store-listing refresh |

**Critical lead-times** the content team has to honor (working back from go-live):
- Battle Pass season: 6 weeks lead-time on the season hero kit, 4 weeks on cosmetic + pet, 2 weeks on copy/banner art.
- Realm Reckoning: 5 weeks lead-time on biome art + boss kit, 3 weeks on the realm pet, 1 week on event-store balance.
- Long Dawn chapter swap: 3 weeks lead-time on banner + sub-quest scripts.
- TOL / BAN / TRN: 1 week lead-time (re-uses existing combat content; only the modifier set, banner art, and reward table change).

If content slips, the substitution priority is:
1. Re-run prior Realm Reckoning art with a buff modifier.
2. Extend Banner Festival to 7 days instead of adding new content.
3. Slip the Collab slot, fill with an expanded RR (the slot has soft commitments since IP deals are uncertain).
