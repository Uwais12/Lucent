# Sigils — Rune Catalog for Lucent: Shards of the Shattered Sun

> **Power-budget share:** 8% of total character power (per `design/04-progression-and-economy.md` §2).
> **Slots:** 3 active Sigil slots, unlocked at account level 8 / 20 / 40.
> **Currency:** Sigil Dust (upgrade), Gems (reroll fee), Chaos Catalyst (rarity push), Gold (light secondary cost).
> **Categories:** 4 — Aim, Body, Spark, Bond. 6 Sigils each. 24 total at launch.
> **Reference:** Archero 2 rune system (`research/games/archero-2.md` §6.1) with Enchantium → Sigil Dust rename and a more legible 4-pillar split.

In-fiction, a **Sigil** is a fragment of light-glyph crystallized from a fallen Shard. Bearers etch it onto their forearm or quiver; the inscription glows faintly when its conditions activate. Sigils are the *personal craft* of a Lucent-bearer — they are how a hero specializes their inherited shard. Sigil Dust is the powdered remainder from Sigils that did not survive a Dim incursion, recovered from corrupted ground.

## 1. The Four Categories

| Category | Identity | Stat domain | Visual motif |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aim Sigils** | Projectile precision | crit rate, crit dmg, atk speed, atk range, accuracy | Tight crosshair-glyph, gold/white glow |
| **Body Sigils** | Defensive resilience | HP, armor, dodge, lifesteal, shield | Heart-glyph in deep indigo |
| **Spark Sigils** | Raw offense | ATK, boss dmg, elite dmg, low-HP dmg | Jagged starburst, red-violet glow |
| **Bond Sigils** | Utility & companions | energy regen, gold find, XP gain, pet dmg, sprite chance | Linked-ring glyph, prismatic shimmer |

Each category supports a clear set-bonus identity (§5) and ladders into specific build archetypes (§7).

## 3. Per-Level Upgrade Costs

Sigil Dust + Gold to take a Sigil from level *n* → *n+1*. Cost scales with rarity (higher tiers ask more dust per step) and with level band (steeper near the cap to preserve the "next milestone is meaningful" feel from Pillar 3).

| Level band | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0 → +5 | 40 dust + 1k gold/lvl | 80 + 2k | 160 + 4k | 320 + 8k | 640 + 16k |
| +6 → +10 | 80 + 2k | 160 + 4k | 320 + 8k | 640 + 16k | 1,280 + 32k |
| +11 → +15 | 160 + 4k | 320 + 8k | 640 + 16k | 1,280 + 32k | 2,560 + 64k |
| +16 → +20 | 320 + 8k | 640 + 16k | 1,280 + 32k | 2,560 + 64k | 5,120 + 128k |

**Total +0 → +20 cost** (sum of 20 steps):

| Rarity | Sigil Dust | Gold |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 3,000 | 75k |
| Rare | 6,000 | 150k |
| Epic | 12,000 | 300k |
| Legendary | 24,000 | 600k |
| **Mythic** | **48,000** | **1.2M** |

Mythic is the load-bearing number for §8 (faucet/sink balance).

## 4. Slot System

Sigils don't slot freely — each consumes **capacity** based on rarity. Capacity grows with account level so that the player's slot choices stay tense for the whole game (per Pillar 3, "next goal three levels away").

### 4.1 Active slots

| Slot | Unlocks at | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Slot 1 | Account Lv 8 | First gated reward after the tutorial Vale; introduces the Sigils UI. |
| Slot 2 | Account Lv 20 | Aligns with first Tower unlock; signals "you have a build now." |
| Slot 3 | Account Lv 40 | Pairs with Mythic-gear feasibility; opens 3-Sigil set bonuses (§5). |

### 4.2 Capacity cost per Sigil

| Rarity | Capacity cost |
|---|---|
| Common | 1 |
| Rare | 2 |
| Epic | 3 |
| Legendary | 4 |
| Mythic | 5 |

### 4.3 Capacity by account level

| Account band | Total capacity | Example loadouts |
|---|---|---|
| Lv 8–19 | **6** | 1 Mythic + 1 Common; 2 Epic; 3 Rare; 1 Legendary + 1 Rare |
| Lv 20–39 | **9** | 1 Mythic + 1 Epic + 1 Common; 3 Epic; 1 Legendary + 1 Epic + 1 Rare |
| Lv 40–59 | **12** | 2 Mythic + 1 Rare; 3 Epic + 1 Legendary; 1 Mythic + 1 Legendary + 1 Epic |
| Lv 60+ | **15** | 3 Mythic; 3 Legendary + 1 Common (over-cap, illegal); 1 Mythic + 2 Legendary + 1 Common (over-cap, illegal) |

> The 15-capacity tier is intentionally just shy of "3 Mythics + a Common." You can run 3 Mythics, but not 3 Mythics plus a free filler — the choice between *one perfect loadout* and *flex coverage* stays alive at endgame.

### 4.4 Loadout legality rule

A loadout is legal iff `sum(capacity_cost) ≤ current_capacity` AND `len(slotted) ≤ unlocked_slots`. The UI shows both meters and disables the Confirm button on red.

## 5. Set Bonuses

When **multiple Sigils of the same category** are slotted together, they unlock category-themed set bonuses. Because the launch cap is 3 slots, the **3-of-category bonus is the live ceiling** at launch. **6-of-category bonuses are reserved for the post-launch slot expansion** (a planned account-level-80 unlock or a Resonance-style mod that lets a slot host two Sigils).

### 5.1 3-of-Category Bonuses (launch live)

| Category | 3-piece bonus | Build effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Aim** | +15% crit rate, +25% crit dmg, projectiles pierce 1 additional enemy | "Crit-Pierce" identity — turns crit builds from streaky to dominant. |
| **Body** | +20% max HP, +10% lifesteal, gain a 1-time per-room shield = 8% max HP | Sustain identity — turns Wardlight/Wraithsworn into raid-grade frontline. |
| **Spark** | +25% ATK, +15% dmg vs Boss enemies, +15% dmg vs Elites | Pure damage identity — the "boss-killer" archetype. |
| **Bond** | +30% gold find, +25% XP gain, +25% pet/sprite damage, +1 sprite-spawn at run start | Utility/companion identity — feeds Sprite and Pet build families. |

### 5.2 6-of-Category Bonuses (post-launch, design-locked now to forestall power-creep)

| Category | 6-piece bonus (locked design) |
|---|---|
| **Aim** | All projectiles ricochet once; crits ricochet twice. |
| **Body** | While shielded, attacks deal +30% damage; shield refreshes every 10s. |
| **Spark** | Below 30% HP, all damage is doubled and you cannot drop below 1 HP for 5s once per run. |
| **Bond** | +1 active Pet slot; sprite damage scales with player ATK; gold drops are doubled. |

These will roll out when capacity allows 6-Sigil loadouts (account Lv 80+ or via a "Twin-Etch" Sigil mod). They're written down now so balance-curves agents don't accidentally power-creep into them.

## 6. Enchantment & Rarity Push

Sigils have two endgame mutation paths, mirroring Archero 2's Enchantium reroll plus our own Chaos Catalyst tier-push.

### 6.1 Reroll the affix value (Enchantment)

- **Cost:** 500 Sigil Dust + 50 Gems per reroll.
- **Effect:** Re-rolls the affix value *within its current rarity tier's range* — i.e., a Rare Sigil rolled at the low end of its band can be pushed toward the high end.
- **Mechanic:** Each rarity has a 70–100% range on its base affix value (the catalog numbers in §2 are the *roll-100 high*). A reroll pulls a new value uniformly from the tier's range; the player accepts or rejects the result before paying again.
- **Cap:** Unlimited rerolls. (No "lockout" — this is a long-tail whale sink and a satisfying F2P weekly goal.)
- **Pity:** After 10 rejected rerolls, the next reroll is guaranteed ≥85% of the tier range (mercy rule against streaky RNG).

### 6.2 Push to next rarity (Chaos Catalyst)

- **Cost:** 1 **Chaos Catalyst** + 5,000 Sigil Dust + Gold scaling with target rarity.
- **Effect:** Promotes a Sigil one rarity tier (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → **Mythic+**).
- **Mythic+** is the soft-prestige cap: caps at Mythic+5, each + adds a flat 5% to the Sigil's base affix and unlocks one cosmetic glyph color. Beyond Mythic+5 the catalyst is rejected.
- **Catalyst sources:** Chaos Catalysts drop from Tower milestones every 50 floors, weekly Seal Battle leaderboard top-25%, and the Battle Pass premium track (~2 per season). **Catalysts are never sold directly for cash** (Pillar 5).

### 6.3 Salvage

- Salvaging a Sigil returns **25% of all Dust spent** on its levels plus a small rarity-scaled flat dust amount (Common 50, Rare 100, Epic 250, Legendary 600, Mythic 1500). Catalyst-pushed rarity is *not* refunded — pushing is a permanent decision.

## 8. Faucet / Sink Balance — Sigil Dust

The defining economic question: **how long does it take an F2P player to fully upgrade their endgame Sigil loadout, and is that timeline congruent with the time-to-cap targets in `design/04-progression-and-economy.md` §3?**

### 8.1 Daily inflow target (F2P, fully engaged dailies)

| Source | Sigil Dust per day | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 6 daily quests (full clear) | 250 | Baseline; one quest specifically rewards Dust. |
| Free chest (4× ad-watch) | 80 (4 × 20) | Per `design/04-progression-and-economy.md` §6 ad slots. |
| Daily Tower run rewards | 100 | First Tower clear of the day, all blocks. |
| 2× Seal Battle clears | 80 | Daily weekly-boss attempts. |
| Battle Pass tier-up share | 40 | Smoothed daily contribution from the 45-day pass. |
| **F2P daily total** | **~550 Sigil Dust** | Without weekly boss / event spikes. |

Weekly spike sources add ~2,000 dust (weekly missions, Seal Battle leaderboard, guild boss). **F2P weekly total ≈ 5,850 dust → ~835/day blended.**

Payers (Monthly Card + Battle Pass Premium) get roughly **+40% inflow** (extra ad-free Tower runs, gem→energy conversion, exclusive quests). Privilege Card adds a daily +50 Dust trickle.

### 8.2 Cost to upgrade one Mythic Sigil from +0 → +20

Per §3, **48,000 Sigil Dust + 1.2M gold**.

At F2P 835 Dust/day blended, that's **~57 days** of *all dust* funneled to one Mythic. Realistically players spread upgrades across the loadout, so the player-experienced timeline for "first full Mythic Sigil at +20" is:

- **F2P:** ~90 days, assuming they upgrade only one Sigil priority at a time (the rest sit at +5 to +10).
- **Payer (Monthly Card + BP Premium):** ~50 days.
- **Whale (multi-pack):** ~25 days.

### 8.3 Cost to upgrade a full 3-Mythic endgame loadout

3 × 48,000 = **144,000 Sigil Dust + 3.6M gold**, plus rerolls and Catalyst pushes (a realistic player spends ~30,000 extra dust on rerolls across the loadout's lifetime).

- **F2P time-to-cap on Sigils:** ~**6–7 months** of dailies. Aligns with the Mythic-gear timeline (~3 months) plus padding — Sigils intentionally lag gear so the player has a "still climbing" lane after their gear is mostly maxed.
- This matches `design/04-progression-and-economy.md` §3's "First Mythic gear ~3 months" target and the "Year 1 player still has clear next goals" Pillar 3 commitment.

### 8.4 Sink-side: where does Dust *go* in the live economy?

| Sink | Lifetime spend share (F2P, year 1) |
|---|---|
| Sigil leveling (3× Mythic to +20) | ~70% |
| Reroll Enchantments | ~18% |
| Chaos Catalyst pushes (Dust component) | ~7% |
| Catalog-completion sub-grinds (off-meta Sigils) | ~5% |

### 8.5 Faucet/sink invariant check

- Daily inflow (F2P): ~835 dust.
- Daily theoretical sink (mid-game player upgrading one Mythic): ~750 dust.
- **Healthy ratio: 1.1× inflow vs sink** → players accumulate a small surplus, never feel bricked, but never coast.
- Endgame players (everything maxed) develop a Dust surplus that becomes the natural sink-cliff for the *next* content drop (new Sigil category, slot-4 unlock, Mythic+ pushes). This is intentional — it preserves "Mythic ceilings, friendly floors" (Pillar 3) and gives the LiveOps team a known reservoir to design against.

### 8.6 Anti-stockpile guardrails

- **Sigil Dust soft cap:** 50,000 (UI warns, no hard cap). Above the soft cap, daily quest dust drops convert to a small Gold bonus instead — a gentle nudge to spend.
- **No Dust IAP at launch.** Dust is exclusively a play-faucet currency. Gems can convert to Dust at a weekly-capped rate (250 Gems → 1,000 Dust, capped at 4×/week). This caps whale acceleration to roughly 2× F2P speed on Sigils, preserving Pillar 5 ("payers compress by 2–4×, not 10×").

## 9. UI / UX Commitments

Per Pillar 8 (mobile-portrait readability):

- Sigil category is signaled by color: gold/white (Aim), indigo (Body), red-violet (Spark), prismatic (Bond).
- Loadout screen shows two meters (slot count + capacity) and a Set Bonus banner when a 3-of-category condition is met.
- Rarity frame matches equipment rarity colors (Common white, Rare green, Epic blue, Legendary purple, Mythic gold).
- Affix-roll range shown as a bar with the current roll marked; rerolling animates the marker so the player *sees* the gamble.
- "Suggested loadout" button pre-fills the §7 recommendation but requires confirmation.

4. The 6-of-category bonuses (§5.2) are locked. Re-cost when slot expansion ships, relative to Spirits and Resonance.
